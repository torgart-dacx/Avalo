# ⚔️ Avalo

### A tiny fantasy world is dying. Grab a weapon and see how long you last.

**▶ Play right now (no install, runs in your browser): <https://torgart-dacx.github.io/Avalo/>**

---

Hey — thanks for stopping by. **Avalo** is a fast, browser-based action RPG you can jump into in about three seconds. No downloads, no account, no setup. Click the link, pick a hero, start swinging.

Here's the pitch: the seven runic Seals that kept the Abyss locked have shattered, the sky over Avalo has turned blood-red, and twisted creatures are pouring out faster than anyone can count. You're the last of the **Keepers of Dawn** — the only person left standing between the dark and whatever's behind you. Every enemy you cut down makes you a little stronger... but the horde grows quicker than you do. **How many waves can you survive?**

## Pick your style

- **⚔ Swordsman** — get up close and personal. Spinning blade combos, a wave of steel, and a rage shield for when things get hairy.
- **✦ Mage** — rain destruction from a distance. Meteors, chain lightning, frost blasts, and a literal storm of fire.
- **🏹 Archer** — fast, slippery, and deadly. Rapid-fire and ricocheting arrows, a deadeye shot, and an arrow downpour.

## What you'll actually do

- **Level up and build your hero.** Every level you choose a new upgrade, and the skill trees go *deep* — your Swordsman and your friend's Swordsman can end up playing completely differently.
- **Chase loot.** Gear drops in six rarities, from plain old Common all the way up to **Divine**. Better gear, bigger numbers, more chaos.
- **Unlock 75 achievements** that stick with you. Pull off enough of them and you earn permanent bonuses that carry into your next run — so even a run that ends badly still moves you forward.
- **Fight 15 kinds of enemies** that don't all play fair: some charge you, some split in two when they die, some blow up in your face, some heal their buddies, some teleport right on top of you. And then there are the bosses.

## Three ways to play

- **Arcade** — endless waves, every fifth one a boss. Pure "how far can I get."
- **Dungeon** — clear room after room until you reach the final boss.
- **Open World** — a big map to roam, monsters wandering around, and a threat level that keeps creeping up the longer you stay.

## Controls

`WASD` move · `Left click` attack · `Q / E / R` abilities · `X` ultimate (from level 10) · `I` inventory · `P` pause · `Right click` to pick an upgrade when you level up.
